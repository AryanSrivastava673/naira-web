FAQ

Questions people ask before they trust a free tool

Can you make a design that isn't in the studio?

Yes. Burger, rocket, your mascot, your logo, anything. Send your idea, logo or a photo you like on WhatsApp at +91 92253 44169. We share the design and any cost in chat before we start.

Do these QR codes expire?

No. These are static QR codes. The link is written into the code itself, so it keeps working forever, even if you never visit this page again. Just make sure the link it opens stays live.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No watermark, no trial, no scan limits. We ask for your email once to unlock downloads, and we may send you the occasional tip for growing your business. That's the whole catch.

Do you store my logo or Wi-Fi password?

No. The studio runs entirely in your browser. Your logo, links and passwords are never uploaded to our servers. Only your email is saved when you sign up.

Will an animal or Logo Art QR really scan?

Yes, if the scan test shows green. We test every design 4 ways, including small and blurry, before you download. For Logo Art, slide towards "Easy to scan" or press "Make it scan better" if the meter is low. Always test one print with your phone before printing hundreds.

What size should I print it?

At least 2.5 cm wide for a table or card. For posters, about one tenth of the scanning distance, so 10 cm for someone scanning from 1 metre. Print animal and Logo Art designs a bit bigger, around 5 to 8 cm.

Can I change the link after printing?

Not with a static QR, the link is fixed. Tip: point the QR to a page you control, like your own website or Naira menu link, then update that page whenever you want.

Can I use these commercially?

Yes. Menus, packaging, posters, cards, ads. The design and the code are yours.
