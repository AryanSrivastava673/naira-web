Questions

How does a Google review NFC card work?

The card has a small NFC chip and a QR code. When a guest taps their phone on it, or scans the QR, your Google review page opens. No app needed.

Will it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. iPhone XS and newer and most Android phones read it with a tap. Older phones scan the QR on the back, so every guest is covered.

What is the price of a Google review card in India?

Our Google review NFC card is ₹399, the NFC + QR standee is ₹749, and the Counter Kit (standee + review card + Wi-Fi card) is ₹1,199. Shipping is charged at actuals.

Can I put my own logo and colours on it?

Yes. Pick a design above or tell us your own idea. We send a design proof on WhatsApp and print only after you say yes.

What if my Wi-Fi password changes?

We can rewrite the NFC chip with the new password. The printed QR needs a reprint, so pick a guest password you don't plan to change often.

Do you deliver outside Pune?

Yes, across India. Shipping is added at actual courier cost.
