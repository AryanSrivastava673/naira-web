Questions

Quick answers.

Do guests need an app?

No. The phone camera reads the QR, and NFC opens the menu with a tap. Nothing to install.

Does NFC work on every phone?

Most phones from the last few years have NFC. Every piece also has a QR, so nobody is left out.

Can I use only QR menus?

Yes. QR stands and stickers work alone and fit every budget. You get the same smart menu.

Will my custom QR really scan?

Yes. We test every design on iPhone and Android, at the size it will be printed.

How do I change prices or items?

From your phone. Changes show on every table at once. No reprint.
